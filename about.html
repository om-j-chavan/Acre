The studio.

A small story about how Acre came to be, and the people whose work fills it.

Acre began with a single terracotta pot — bought from a maker in a quiet village, carried home in a suitcase, and placed on a sunlit windowsill. It became, over time, a small obsession with the way honest objects can change a room.

The pieces we sell now come from independent makers we've met, written to, visited, and slowly grown to know. There is no algorithm to this. We choose what we love, and what we believe will outlast us.

— Eleanor & James, founders

Our makers

Hands first, machines second.

We work with potters in Portugal, blacksmiths in northern Italy, weavers in Nepal, and a small handful of Melbourne studios. Most of our pieces are made in batches of fewer than a hundred. When something sells out, it returns when the maker is ready — not before.
